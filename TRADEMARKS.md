This is a plain-language policy, not legal advice. The maintainers may update it; the version in `main` applies.

## Allowed without permission

- Saying truthfully that your product works with, imports from, or is compatible with Pagefirst ("Works with Pagefirst", "Imports Pagefirst sites").
- Naming Pagefirst in articles, reviews, talks, tutorials and academic work.
- Running Pagefirst yourself, internally or for your own sites, and saying you do.
- Distributing an unmodified copy of Pagefirst under the Apache License with its names and notices intact.
- Using the name in the repository's fork and pull request workflow, for example a fork named `yourname/pagefirst`.
- Using the plain-text name (not the logo) in a way that is accurate and does not suggest endorsement.

## Needs written permission

- Offering a hosted or managed service, or a commercial support or consulting service, under a name that is or contains "Pagefirst" or "Page First", or a confusingly similar name.
- Using the Marks in a company, product, domain, package, app-store or social account name.
- Using the logos, or a modified or similar logo, in any way other than the accurate reference above.
- Distributing a modified version under the Pagefirst name. A modified version must use a different name and say that it is based on Pagefirst.
- Any use that suggests Pagefirst sponsors, endorses or is affiliated with you.

## Self-hosted instances

If you self-host Pagefirst, you may keep the default "Pagefirst" branding in the product interface. If you offer your instance to third parties as a service, rebrand it or ask for permission first.

## Enterprise features

Code in the separately licensed enterprise folder is covered by its own license. This policy applies to the Marks regardless of which code you run.

## Asking for permission or reporting misuse
